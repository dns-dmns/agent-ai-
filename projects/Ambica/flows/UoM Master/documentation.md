# UoM Master Functional Flow

## Overview
The UoM (Unit of Measure) Master workflow enables management of standardized units across the enterprise system.

## Step-by-Step Recorded Execution
1. **Navigate to Master Data**: Click on 'Master Data' sidebar menu.
2. **Open UoM Master**: Click 'UoM Master' sub-menu item.
3. **Initiate Add UoM**: Click 'ADD UOM' button to open modal.
4. **Input UoM Name**: Enter unit name in `#uomName` text box.
5. **Submit Data**: Click 'Submit' button to persist changes.
6. **View/Edit Record**: Click grid action buttons to review or modify details.
7. **Delete Record**: Click action button to delete unnecessary UoMs.
