# Customer Master Module QA Documentation

## Overview
The Customer Master module allows administrative users to view, create, edit, and delete customer entity records.

## Workflow Steps
1. **Navigate**: Master Data -> Customer Master.
2. **Add Customer**: Click 'Add Customer', fill in Party Code, Name, Country, State, Region, and submit.
3. **View/Edit**: Click record row buttons to open details view or update details.
4. **Delete**: Click delete action button and confirm via popup.
